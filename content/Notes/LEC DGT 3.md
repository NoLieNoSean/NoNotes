[!Definition] smooth maps between manifolds

[!Lemma] Well-definedness of previous definition

---

Suppose that $X\subseteq \mathbb{R}^{N}$ is a $k$-manifold and that $\phi:U\to X$ is a local parameterization around $x$, and assume $\phi(0)=x$ for convenience. Define the **tangent space** $T_{x}(X)$of $X$ at $x$ be to be image of the map $d\phi_{0}:\mathbb{R}^{k}\to \mathbb{R}^{N}$ (this is well defined and does not depend on $\phi$). The parallel translate $x+T_{x}(X)$ is the closest flat approximation to $X$ through $x$.
