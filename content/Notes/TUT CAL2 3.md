[!Theorem]
Let $U\subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be open. $\mathbf{f}:U\to \mathbb{R}^{m}$ be $C^{1}$ and $\mathbf{a}\in U$ such that $\text{rank}(\mathbf{D}\mathbf{f}(\mathbf{a}))=r$. Show that there exist neighborhoods $V_{1}$ of $a$ (in $U$), $W_{1}$ of $f(a)$ , $V_{2}$ of $\mathbf{0}$ in $\mathbb{R}^{n}$, and $W_{2}$ of $\mathbf{0}$ in $\mathbb{R}^{m}$ along with $C_{1}$ diffeomorphisms $\varphi:V_{1}\to V_{2}$ and $\psi:W_{1}\to W_{2}$ such that $\varphi(a)=\mathbf{0}$, and $\psi(\mathbf{f}(\mathbf{a}))=\mathbf{0}$, and $\psi \circ f\circ\varphi ^{-1}(x_{1}, x_{2}, \dots, x_{n})=(x_{1}, \dots, x_{r}, 0,0,\dots,0)$.
