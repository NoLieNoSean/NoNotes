Examples
1. If $X$ is a tree, then $X$ is $\text{CAT}(\kappa)$ for any $\kappa\leqslant 0$. 

If $X_{1}$, $X_{2}$ are $\text{CAT}(\kappa)$ spaces for $k\leqslant 0$, then $X_{1}\times X_{2}$ is $\text{CAT}(0)$. 

[!Theorem]
If $X$ is $\text{CAT}(0)$, then the distance function $d:X\times X\to \mathbb{R}$ is convex: If $\sigma_{1}, \sigma_{2}:I\to \mathbb{R}$ are two geodesic segments parameterized by arc length, we have 
$$
d(\sigma_{1}(t), \sigma_{2}(t))\leqslant (1-t)d(\sigma_{1}(0), \sigma_{2}(0))+td(\sigma_{1}(1), \sigma_{2}(1)).
$$
