# M-alternating paths

$M$-alternating path proof for Hall's. [[Introduction to Graph Theory (Douglas B. West).pdf#page=131|ref]]

> [!Theorem]
> A [[DMAT_L21#Matchings|matching]] $M$ in a graph $G$ is a maximum matching in $G$ iff $G$ has no $M$-augmenting path.
