Equality axioms, prime formulas (formulas beginning with $\exists$ or $\forall$), prime formula structure (the prop. logic formula obtained by treating all prime formulas as atomic proposition).

[!Lemma]
Let $\mathcal{I}$ be an interpretation $(\mathcal{M}, \sigma)$. Then there exists a truth valuation $V$ for the set of prime formulas such that for all FO formulas $\varphi$, $\mathcal{I}⊫_{FO}\varphi$ iff $V⊫_{PL}pfr(\varphi)$.
