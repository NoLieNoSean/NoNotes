# Ping pong lemma
> [!Lemma] Ping pong lemma
> Let $G_{1}, G_{2}\leqslant G$ with $|G_{1}|\geqslant 3$, $|G_{2}|\geqslant 2$. Suppose $G$ acts on a set $X$, with nonempty $X_{1}, X_{2}\subseteq X$ such that $g_{2}(X_{1})\subseteq X_{2}$, $g_{1}(X_{2})\subseteq X_{1}$ for all $g_{i}\in G_{i}\setminus \{ 1 \}$, $i=1, 2$, and $X_{1}\cap X_{2}=\emptyset$. Then the subgroup generated by $G_{1}\cup G_{2}$ is isomorphic to the free product $G_{1}*G_{2}$. 

The proof you know, with alternating words that start and end with elements of $G_{1}$. Other cases can be reduced to this by conjugating by suitable elements of $G_{1}$ (hence the $|G_{1}|\geqslant 3$ requirement). 

Variations: Suppose there exists $p\in X$ such that $p\not\in X_{i}$, with $g_{1}(X_{2}\cup \{ p \})\subseteq X_{1}$, $g_{2}(X_{1}\cup \{ p \})\subseteq X_{2}$, then $\langle G_{1}\cup G_{2} \rangle\cong G_{1}*G_{2}$. No further assumption on $X_{1}$ and $X_{2}$ is needed.
