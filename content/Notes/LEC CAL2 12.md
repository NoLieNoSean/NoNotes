Let $v_{1}, \dots, v_{n}$ be a basis of $V$, $\omega\in\bigwedge^{n}V$. $\{ v_{1}^{*}\wedge\dots \wedge v_{n}^{*} \}$ is a basis of $\bigwedge^{n}V$. Show that $\omega=\omega(v_{1}, \dots, v_{n})(v_{1}^{*}\wedge\dots \wedge v_{n}^{*})$.
