# Symplectic reduction and moment polytope

[!Example]
Let $M=\mathbb{C}^{n}$, $\omega=\sum_{I=1}^{n}dz_{I}\wedge d\overline{z}_{I}$. 
Consider 'action-angle variables' $(I_{I}, \theta_{I})$ as $z_{I}=\sqrt{ I_{I} }e^{\theta_{I}}$ for all $I$. Then, $\omega=\sum_{a=1}^{n}dI_{a}d\theta_{a}$. 

Consider $T^{n}$ action on $(\mathbb{C}^{n}, \omega)$ as $\boldsymbol{\alpha}(\mathbf{I}, \boldsymbol{\theta})=(\mathbf{I}, \boldsymbol{\theta}+\boldsymbol{\alpha})$. 

The moment map $\mu:\mathbb{C}^{n}\to(\mathbb{R}^{n})^{*}\cong \mathbb{R}^{n}$.
