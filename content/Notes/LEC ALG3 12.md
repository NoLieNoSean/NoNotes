gcd not defined in power series ring

If $R$ is a UFD, when is primitive $f(x)\in R[x]$ irreducible in $R[x]$?
Since $R$ is an ID, its ring of fractions $Q$ is a field, so irreducible elements are prime.
$f(x)$ is irreducible in $R[x]$ $\iff$ $f(x)$ is irreducible in $Q[x]$. 

---

[!Lemma]
Let $R$ be a UFD and $f(x)\in R[x]$ be a primitive polynomial. Then $f(x)$ is irreducible in $R[x]$ $\iff$ $f(x)$ is irreducible in $Q[x]$. 

[!Proof]-
$(\implies)$ done

$(\impliedby)$ obvious.

[!Theorem]
If $R$ is a UFD, then $R[x]$ is a UFD.
