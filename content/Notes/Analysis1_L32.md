## Functions defined by a power series

Functions from $\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathbb{C}$ can be defined using [[Analysis1_L31#Power series|power series]]. In the complex case, we have
$$
\begin{align}
 & f:\{ z \in \mathbb{C}: |z| <R \}\to \mathbb{C} ,\\  & f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_{n}z^{n}.
\end{align}
$$
$R$ is the radius of convergence of the power series, as defined in the previous lecture.
