# Proper actions

> [!Definition]
> 
> Given a metric space $X$ and an action $\Phi:\Gamma\to \text{Homeo}(X)$, one says that $\Gamma$ **acts by isometries** on $X$ if $\mathrm{im}\,\Phi \subseteq \text{Isom}(X)$. 
> 
> Let $\Gamma$ be a group *acting by isometries* on a *metric space* $X$. The action is said to be **proper** if for each $x\in X$ there exists a number $r> 0$ such that the set $\{ \gamma\in\Gamma:\gamma B(x, r)\cap B(x, r)\ne \emptyset \}$ is finite. 
> 
> An action $\Phi$ is said to be **cocompact** if there exists a compact set $K\subseteq X$ such that $X=\Gamma.K$. 

^98ccf1


Given an action of a group $\Gamma$ on a space $X$, we write $\Gamma_{x}$ for the stabilizer of $x\in X$.

> [!Proposition]
> Suppose $\Gamma$ acts properly by isometries on a metric space $X$. Then, every compact subset $K\subseteq X$ has an open neighborhood $U$ such that $\{ \gamma\in\Gamma:\gamma U\cap U\ne \emptyset \}$ is finite. 



> [!Exercise]
> Let $X$ be a length space. If there is a group that acts properly and cocompactly by isometries on $X$, then $X$ is complete and locally compact, so by the [[LEC GGT 9#^142a7d|Hopf-Rinow Theorem]] it is a proper geodesic space. 

> [!Proposition]
> Suppose that the group $\Gamma$ acts *properly by isometries* on the *metric space* $X$. Then:
> 1. For each $x\in X$, there exist $\epsilon> 0$ such that if $\gamma B(x, \epsilon)\cap B(x, \epsilon)\ne \emptyset$ then $\gamma\in\Gamma_{x}$. 
> 2. The distance between orbits in $X$ defines a metric on the orbit space $X/\Gamma$. 
> 3. If the action is free, then the natural projection $p:X\to X/\Gamma$ is a covering map and a local isometry. 
> 4. If a subspace $Y$ of $X$ is invariant under the action of a subgroup $H\subseteq\Gamma$, then the action of $H$ on $Y$ is proper. 
> 5. If the action of $\Gamma$ is *cocompact* then there are only finitely many conjugacy classes of isotropy subgroups in $\Gamma$. 


> [!Proposition]
> Let $X$ be a *length space* that is connected and simply connected, and suppose that the group $\Gamma \subseteq \text{Isom}(X)$ acts freely and properly on $X$. Consider $X/\Gamma$ with the induced metric. 
> 
> Denote the normalizer of $\Gamma$ in $\text{Isom}(X)$ by $N(\Gamma)$. There is a natural isomorphism $N(\Gamma)/\Gamma\cong \text{Isom}(X/\Gamma)$, induced by the map $v\mapsto \overline{v}$, where $v\in N(\Gamma)$ and $\overline{v}\in \text{Isom}(X/\Gamma)$ is the map $\Gamma x  \mapsto\Gamma(v(x))$. 

# Group of isometries of a compact metric space

> [!Definition]
> Let $Y$ be a compact metric space. One defines a metric on the group $\text{Isom}(Y)$ by
> $$
> d(\alpha, \alpha'):=\sup _{y\in Y}d(\alpha y, \alpha'y).
> $$
> 

^357b22

> [!Proposition]
> The metric on $\text{Isom}(Y)$ defined in [[#^357b22]] is invariant by left and right translations. With the topology induced by this metric, $\text{Isom}(Y)$ is a compact [[Topological groups#^f20c16|topological group]]. 


# Presenting groups of Homeomorphisms

> [!Theorem] @bridsonMetricSpacesNonpositive1999 8.10
> Let $X$ be a topological space, let $\Gamma$ be a group acting on $X$ by homeomorphisms, and let $U\subseteq X$ be an open subset such that $X=\Gamma U$. 
> 1. If $X$ is connected, then the set $S=\{ \gamma\in\Gamma:\gamma U\cap U\ne \emptyset \}$ generates $\Gamma$. 
> 2. Let $\mathcal{A}_{S}$ be a set of symbols $a_{s}$ indexed by $S$. If $X$ and $U$ are both path connected and $X$ is simply connected, then $\Gamma=\langle \mathcal{A}_{S}\ | \ \mathcal{R} \rangle$, where
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \mathcal{R}=\{ a_{s_{1}}a_{s_{2}}a_{s_{3}}^{-1}\ | \  s_{i}\in S , U\cap s_{1}U\cap s_{3}U\ne \emptyset, s_{1}s_{2}=s_{3} \text{ in }\Gamma\}.
> \end{align}
> $$
> 
> > [!Proof]-
> > 
> > $(1)$ Let $H\subseteq\Gamma$ be the subgroup of $\Gamma$ generated by $S$. Let $V=HU$ and let $V'=(\Gamma \setminus H)U$. If $V\cap V'\ne \emptyset$, then there exist $h\in H$, $h'\in\Gamma \setminus H$ such that $h^{-1}h'U\cap U\ne \emptyset$ and hence $h'\in H$, contrary to assumption. Thus the open sets $V$ and $V'$ are disjoint. $V$ is non-empty and $X$ is connected, therefore $V'=\emptyset$ and $H=\Gamma$. 



> [!Corollary]
> A group is finitely presented iff it [[#^98ccf1|acts properly and cocompactly by isometries]] on a simply connected [[LEC GGT 8#^11d791|geodesic space]].
