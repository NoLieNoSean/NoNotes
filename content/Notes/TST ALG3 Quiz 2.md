# Problem 4

Let $k$ be a field and let $R=k[x_{1}, \dots, x_{d}]$ be a polynomial ring. Let $r, d\geqslant 2$ and $f(x_{1}, \dots, x_{d})=x_{1}^{r}+x_{2}^{r}+\dots+x_{d}^{r}$. 

> [!Exercise]
> Let $d=r=3$. Is $f$ irreducible? Does it depend on the characteristic of the field?
