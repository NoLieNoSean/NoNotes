## Third isomorphism theorem

> [!Theorem] Third Isomorphism Theorem
> Let $G$ be a group and let $H\triangleleft G$, $K\triangleleft G$, and $H<K$. Then, $K/H\triangleleft G/H$ and 
> $$
> \frac{{G/H}}{K/H}\cong G/K.
> $$
