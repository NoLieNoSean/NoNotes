# Preliminaries

[[Analytic functions]]


Riemannian metrics, R manifolds, local diffeomorphisms, Every smooth manifold admits a riemannian metric, the space of riemannian metrics on $M$ is infinite dimensional. Whitney embedding theorem (every smooth manifold is diffeomorphic to an embedded submanifold of an Euclidean space), Nash (every riemannian manifold can be embedded into $\mathbb{R}^{N}$ such that $g=\varphi^{*}g_{E}$). Product of manifolds, warp products
