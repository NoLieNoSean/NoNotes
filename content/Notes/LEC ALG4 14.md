[!Proposition]
Let $A$ be a graded ring $A=A_{0}\oplus A_{1}\oplus\dots$. Let $I$ be an $A$ ideal. TFAE:
1. $I$ is generated by homogeneous elements. 
2. $I=\bigoplus_{n\geqslant 0}I\cap A_{n}$ [^1]

Such an ideal is called a **homogeneous ideal**. 

[!Proof]-

$(1\implies 2)$ by definition. 

$(2\implies 1)$ Let $Z=\{ \zeta_{\lambda}:\lambda\in\Lambda \}$ be homogeneous such that $I$ is the ideal generated by $Z$. Let $\eta\in I$. Write $\eta=\sum_{i=1}^{r}a_{i}\zeta_{i}$ with $a_{i}\in A$. For any $b\in A$, we can write $b=\sum_{i\geqslant 0}b_{i}$ with $b_{i}\in A_{i}$ and $b_{i}=0$ for all but finitely many $i$.  

[^1]: Elements of $A_{n}$ are called homogeneous of degree $n$. $(2)$ says that $I=\sum_{n\geqslant 1}I\cap A_{n}$.
