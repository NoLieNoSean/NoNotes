# The Fourier transform

For $f\in L^{1}(\mathbb{R})$, define $\hat{f}:\mathbb{R}\to \mathbb{C}$ by
$$
\hat{f}(\zeta):=\int_{\mathbb{R}}f(x)e^{ -2\pi ix\zeta }\,dx.
$$
For all $\zeta\in \mathbb{R}$, $|\hat{f}(\zeta)|\leqslant \lVert f_{1} \rVert$. Thus, $\hat{f}\in L^{\infty}$, and $\lVert \hat{f} \rVert_{\infty}\leqslant \lVert f \rVert_{1}$.
