> [!Definition] Topological group
> A topological group $G$ is a topological space that is also a group such that the group operation $\cdot:G\times G\to G$, $(x, y)\mapsto xy$ and the inversion map $^{-1}:G\to G$, $x \mapsto  x ^{-1}$ are continuous. Such a topology is said to be compatible with the group operations.

^f20c16

To show that a topology is compatible with the group operations, it suffices to check that the map $G\times G\to G$, $(x, y)\mapsto xy^{-1}$ is continuous.
