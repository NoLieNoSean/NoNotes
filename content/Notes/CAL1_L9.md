> [!Theorem] Extreme value theorem
> Let $X$ be a metric space. Then $X$ is compact if and only if every continuous real valued function on $X$ takes a maximum and a minimum value.

We have already shown the forward implication [[Analysis1_L16#Extreme value theorem|here]].

**Proof of $\impliedby$**
Assume every real valued function on $X$ takes a minimum and maximum value. 


> [!Theorem] Lebesgue covering Lemma
> Let $\{ O_{\lambda} \}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$ be an open cover of a compact metric space $X$. Then there exists $\epsilon> 0$ such that for each $x\in X$, the ball $B_{\epsilon}(x, X)$ is contained in some member of the cover.
