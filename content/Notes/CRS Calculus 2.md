If $F:[0, 1]\times U\to\mathbb{R}$ is continuous, and $G(x)=\int_{0}^{1} F(t, x) \, dx$, then 
$$
\frac{\partial G(x)}{\partial x_{i}}=\int_{0}^{1} \frac{\partial F(t, x)}{\partial x_{i}} \, dt 
$$
