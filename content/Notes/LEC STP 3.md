Let $G$ be a Lie group. 
Let $\mathscr{G}=\text{Lie}(G)=T_{e}G$. 
Let $\mathscr{G}^{*}=\text{dual of }\mathscr{G}$. 

---

Let $G=\mathbb{R}^{3}$ (abelian group of translations)
$G$ acts on $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ by $(\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{x})\mapsto \mathbf{x}+\mathbf{a}$.
