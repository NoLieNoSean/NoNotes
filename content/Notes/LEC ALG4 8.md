Recall:
$M$ torsion module $\implies$ $M=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{m}M(p_{i})$, where $\{ p_{1}, \dots, p_{m} \}$ are the non associate primes which contain $\text{Ann}(M)$. 

Today: $M=M(p)$, $p$ prime. 

> [!Remark]
> Let $M$ be an $R$-module generated by $\{ x_{1}, \dots, x_{n} \}$. Let $I$ be an $R$-ideal. Then $\overline{x}_{1}, \dots, \overline{x}_{n}\in M/IM$ generate $M/IM$ as an $R$ module. Therefore by [[LEC ALG4 3#^b14fe9]], it also generates $M/IM$ as an $R$-module. 

[!Proposition]
Let $x_{1}, \dots, x_{m}\in M$ be such that $\overline{x}_{1}, \dots, \overline{x}_{n}\in M/pM$ generates $M/pM$ as an $R/p$ vector space. Then, $x_{1}, \dots, x_{n}$ generate $M$ as an $R$-module. (may be true with more general hypothesis)

[!Proof]-

Let $N=\sum_{i=1}^{m}Rx_{i}\subseteq M$. We will show that $N=M$. Let $y\in M$. There exist $a_{1}, \dots, a_{m}\in R$ such that $\overline{y}=\sum_{i=1}^{m}a_{i}\overline{x}_{i}$. Write $z=\sum_{i=1}^{m}a_{i}x_{i}$. Then, $y-z\in pM$. Thus, for all $y\in M$, there exists $z\in N$ such that $y-z\in pM$. 

$y-z=py_{1}$, $y_{1}\in M$. 
$y-z-pz_{1}\in p^{2}M$
$y-z-pz_{1}-p^{2}z_{2}\in p^{3}M$
...
$y-z-pz_{1}-p^{2}z_{2}-\dots-p^{e}z_{e}\in p^{e+1}M=0$ Since all $z$'s are in $N$, it follows that $y\in N$. 


[!Corollary]
Every minimal generating set of $M$ has the same cardinality, $\dim_{R/p}M/pM$. 

[!Definition]
A subset $\{ x_{1}, \dots, x_{m} \}\subseteq M$ is called **independent** if for all $a_{1}, \dots, a_{n}$, 
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{m} a_{i}x_{i}=0\implies a_{i}x_{i}=0 \forall i.
$$
