[!Proposition] Riemann-Lebesgue
Let $f$ be a bounded Riemann integrable. Then, $\hat{f}_{n}\to 0$ as $|n|\to \infty$. 

This implies that
$$
\begin{align}
\int f(t)\cos(nt)\,dt\to 0 \\
\int f(t)\sin(nt)\,dt\to 0
\end{align}
$$
as $n\to \infty$.
