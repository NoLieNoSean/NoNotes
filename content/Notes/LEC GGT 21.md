[!Theorem] @bridsonMetricSpacesNonpositive1999 I.8.32


Suppose that $\Gamma=A*_{C}B$. Then there exists a tree $T$ such that $\gamma$ acts via isometries on $T$ properly and cocompactly with fundamental domain an edge such that $\Gamma_{u}=A$, $\Gamma_{v}=B$, $\text{Isotropy}(e)=C$.
