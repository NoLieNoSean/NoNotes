[!Proposition]
Given $p\in M$, there exists open neighborhood $W\subseteq M$ of $p$ and $\delta> 0$ such that for all $q\in W$, $\text{inj}(q)>\delta$ and $W\subseteq B_{\delta}(q)=\exp_{q}(B_{\delta}(0))$. 

[!Theorem]
Let $\gamma:I\to M$ be a geodesic, $t_{0}\in I$. Then there exists $[a, b]\subseteq I$, $t_{0}\in[a, b]$, such that $\gamma_{[a, b]}$ is minimizing. 

[!Theorem]
Let $\gamma:[a, b]\to M$ be a piecewise smooth minimizing curve. Then $\gamma$ is smooth. 

[!Lemma]
Let $\gamma:I\to M$ be minimizing, $J\subseteq I$. Then $\gamma|_{J}$ is also minimizing.
