> [!Definition] Real projective space
> Define $\mathbb{P}^{n}$, the **real projective space** of dimension $n$, to be the set of $1$-dimensional linear subspaces (lines through the origin) in $\mathbb{R}^{n+1}$. There is a natural map $\pi:\mathbb{R}^{n+1}\setminus \{ 0 \}\to \mathbb{P}^{n}$ defined by sending a point $x$ to its span. We topologize $\mathbb{P}^{n}$ by giving it the [[The quotient topology#^4fa85d|quotient topology]] with respect to this map. 

> [!Proposition]
> The real projective space $\mathbb{P}^{n}$ is an $n$-manifold.
