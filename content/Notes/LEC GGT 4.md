[!Definition] Affine connection
An affine connection on $M$ is an $\mathbb{R}$-bilinear map
$$
\nabla : \mathfrak{X}(M)\times \mathfrak{X}(M)\to \mathfrak{X}(M)
$$
such that 
1. $\nabla_{fX}Y=f\nabla_{X}Y$ for all $f\in C^{\infty}(M)$
2. $\nabla_{X}(fY)=X(f)Y+f\nabla_{X}Y$.

In terms of local coordinates,
$$
\nabla_{\partial_{i}}\partial_{j}=\Gamma_{ij}^{k}\partial_{k}.
$$
An affine connection is called symmetric if
$$
\nabla_{X}Y-\nabla_{Y}X=[X, Y]=0.
$$
