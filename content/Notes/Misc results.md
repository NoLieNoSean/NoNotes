> [!Theorem] Multinomial expansion
> $$
> \begin{align}
> (x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{m})^{n}=\sum_{\substack{k_{1}+\dots +k_{m}=n\\\\ k_{1}, \dots, k_{m}\geqslant  0 }}\binom{n}{k_{1}, k_{2}, \dots, k_{m}}x_{1}^{k_{1}}x_{2}^{k_{2}}\dots x_{m}^{k_{m}}.
> \end{align}
> $$
