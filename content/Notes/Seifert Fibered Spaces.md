> [!Definition]
> The **fibered solid torus** of type $(l, m)$ is a solid torus partitioned into circles obtained as follows: Consider the cylinder $\mathbb{D}^{2}\times[-1, 1]$. It is partitioned into intervals of the form $\{ \text{point} \}\times[-1, 1]$. Identify $\mathbb{D}^{2}\times \{ -1 \}$ to $\mathbb{D}^{2}\times \{ 1 \}$ by settings $(re^{ i {2}\pi\theta }, 1)$ equal to $\left( re^{ i 2 \pi \left( \theta + \frac{m}{l} \right) } , -1\right)$, for $m\in \mathbb{N}\cup \{ 0 \}$ and $l\in \mathbb{N}$. A circle formed by intervals of the form $\{ \text{point} \}\times[-1, 1]$ is called a **fiber** of the fibered solid torus. 

A **fiber-preserving** homeomorphism between $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ is a homeomorphism $h:T_{1}\to T_{2}$ that takes fibers to fibers.
