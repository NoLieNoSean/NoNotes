[!Definition]
Basis: union is $X$. Given $x\in B_{1}\cap B_{2}$, $B_{i}\in \mathcal{B}$, there exists $B_{3}\in \mathcal{B}$ such that $x\in B_{3}$. 

Given an abstract basis basis $\mathcal{B}$ in $P(X)$, the topology generated by $\mathcal{B}$ consists of those sets $U$ such that $\forall x\in U$, there exists $B\subseteq \mathcal{B}$ such that $B\subseteq U$. 

This gives a topology
$U$ is open in this topology $\iff$ $Y$ is a union of basis elements. In particular each basis element is open. 

[!Definition]
An abstract subbasis is any subset $S$ of $P(X)$ such that the union of all $V\in S$ is $X$. The associated basis is the set of all finite intersections of elements of $S$. 


Given a topology $T$ and an unrelated basis $\mathcal{B}\subseteq P(X)$, Topology generated by $\mathcal{B}$ is $T$ $\iff$ each $B\in \mathcal{B}$ is open in $T$ and any $U$ in $T$ is a union of elements of $\mathcal{B}$.
