# More radicals

[!Example]
Let $I=(xy, yz, xz)$ in $\mathbb{C}[x,y , z]$. 

Then, $I=(xy, yz, zx)=(x, y)\cap(y, z)\cap(z, x)$, so
$\sqrt{ I }=\sqrt{ (x, y) }\cap \sqrt{ (y, z) }\cap \sqrt{ (z, x) }$.

$I^{2}=(x, y)^{2}\cap(y, z)^{2}\cap(z, x)^{2}\cap(x^{2}, y^{2}, z^{2})$.
$\sqrt{ I^{2} }=\sqrt{ (x, y)^{2} }\cap \sqrt{ (y, z)^{2} }\cap \sqrt{ (z, x)^{2} }\cap \sqrt{ (x^{2}, y^{2}, z^{2}) }$.

[!Lemma]
Let $R=k[x_{1}, \dots, x_{n}]$, where $k$ is a field. Let $I$ and $J$ be ideals generated by monomials. Let $G(I)$ and $G(J)$ denote the generating sets of $I$ and $J$. Then, 
$$
I\cap J=(\{ \text{lcm}(f, g) \ | \  f\in G(I), g\in G(I)\}).
$$





Note: No prime ideal can be written as an intersection of ideals.

#### Existence of maximal ideals

[!Theorem]
Let $R$ be a non-zero ring. Then maximal ideals exist. In fact every ideal is contained in some maximal ideal.
