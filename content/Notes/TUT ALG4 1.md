> [!Exercise] @artinAlgebra2011 Ex. 14.2.3
> Let $A$ be the matrix of a homomorphism $\varphi:\mathbb{Z}^{\oplus n}\to \mathbb{Z}^{\oplus m}$ of [[LEC ALG4 2#^552c68|free]] $\mathbb{Z}$-modules. 
> 1. Prove that $\varphi$ is injective iff the rank of $A$, as a real matrix, is $n$. 
> 2. Prove that $\varphi$ is surjective iff the greatest common divisor of the determinants of the $m\times m$ minors of $A$ is $1$.
