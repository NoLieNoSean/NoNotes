> [!Proposition] @artinAlgebra2011 14.4.9
> Let $A$ be an $m\times n$ matrix, and let $P$ and $Q$ be invertible integer matrices such that $A'=Q^{-1}AP$ has the 'diagonal from'. 
> 1. The integer solutions of the homogeneous equation $A'X'=0$ are the integer vectors $X'$ whose first $k$ coordinates are zero.
> 2. The integer solutions of the homogeneous equation $AX=0$ are those of the form $X=PX'$, where $A'X'=0$. 
> 3. The image $W'$ of multiplication by $A'$ consists of the integer combinations of the vectors $d_{1}w_{2}, \dots, d_{k}e_{k}$. The image of $W$ of multiplication by $A$ consists of the vectors $Y=QY'$, where $Y'$ is in $W'$
