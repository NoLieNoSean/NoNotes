[!Definition] Contact structure
Let $M$ be a $2n+1$ dimensional smooth manifold. A codimension 1 hyperplane field $\xi$ (i.e, a sub[[Fiber bundles#^a3d022|bundle]] of $TM$ of corank 1) is a **contact structure** if for any 1-form $\alpha$ (locally defined) with $\ker\alpha=\xi$, we have $\alpha \wedge(d\alpha)^{n}\ne 0$ everywhere. 

[!Theorem] Frobenius
A hyperplane field given by $\ker\alpha$ is integrable iff $\alpha \wedge d\alpha$ is identically zero.
