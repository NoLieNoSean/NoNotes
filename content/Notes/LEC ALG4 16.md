Let $F$ be a field. Fix an algebraic closure $\overline{F}$ of $F$. 

[!Definition]
Let $\alpha\in \overline{F}$. Say that $\alpha$ is **separable** over $F$ if the minimal polynomial $m_{F}(x)$ of $\alpha$ over $F$ has distinct roots in $\overline{F}$.
