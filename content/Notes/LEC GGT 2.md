[!Proposition]
1. $SL_{2}(\mathbb{R})$ acts on $\mathbb{H}$ transitively via isometries. 
2. The geodesic lines are vertical straight lines and semicircles with center on $\mathbb{R}$. 
3. $\mathbb{H}=SL_{2}(\mathbb{R})/SO(2)$ as a homogeneous space. 
4. If $z_{0}, z_{0}'\in \mathbb{H}$, $z_{1}, z_{1}'\in \mathbb{H}$ and if $d_{\mathbb{H}}(z_{0}, z_{0}')=d_{\mathbb{H}}(z_{1}, z_{1}')$, then there exists $A\in SL_{2}(\mathbb{R})$ such that $Az_{0}=z_{1}$, $Az_{0}=z_{1}'$.
