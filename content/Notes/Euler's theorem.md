Generalizes [[Fermat's little theorem]].

> [!Theorem]
> If $a$ and $n$ are co-prime, then $a^{\phi(n)}\equiv {1} \mod n$.

where $\phi(n)$ is the **Euler totient function**, which counts the number of integers less than or equal to  n  that are co-prime to  n.

Proof every similar to that of Fermat's little theorem.
