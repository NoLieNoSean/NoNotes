# It is easy to opt for the path of least resistance.

It is also easy to to fail to register your growing cognitive bankruptcy until significant damage has already been inflicted.

So, before I'm crippled in my ability to independently express my thoughts and ideas, I'm taking the opportunity to cut AI out of my writing. None of the content on here has seen a prompt box.

<img src="Pasted image 20250619174756.png" class="theme-responsive">

On a lighter note, I miss the pre-2022 era of knowing that everything you encountered on the internet was made by *people*. Once AI matures past its telltale idiosyncrasies (they're not just frustrating — they're *infuriating*!), I cannot imagine how humanity will continue to maintain a collective cultural identity on the internet. I strongly feel that we need places where you can be assured that what you're consuming is a product of human endeavor. This is my measly attempt at contributing to this cause. 

In the meantime, I need some assurances before posting this.
